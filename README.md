## Who I am

I am a Class 10 student at **Masjid Mission Academy (School & College), Rajshahi, Bangladesh**. I am interested in software development, engineering, design, and the practical applications of artificial intelligence.

I like understanding how things work beneath the surface. When I notice a problem, I enjoy researching it, breaking it into smaller parts, and experimenting with possible solutions. I learn by building, testing, getting things wrong, and improving the result.

I am early in my journey, but I take the work seriously. My goal is not simply to learn tools — it is to develop the judgement to choose the right tools, understand the trade-offs, and build things that are genuinely useful.

## How I think

> **Necessity is the mother of invention.**

My approach is simple:

- **Start with a need.** A project should solve a real problem or answer a meaningful question.
- **Understand before automating.** Learn the system, constraints, and trade-offs instead of blindly copying a solution.
- **Keep complexity earned.** Prefer the simplest design that does the job well.
- **Experiment honestly.** Treat failure as feedback, measure what matters, and revise assumptions.
- **Build for people.** A technically impressive solution is not useful if people cannot use it.

**Curiosity gives me questions. Engineering gives me a way to answer them.**
