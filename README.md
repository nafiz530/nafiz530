## About

I am a Class 10 student at **Masjid Mission Academy (School & College), Rajshahi, Bangladesh**, with a strong interest in software development, engineering, design, and the practical applications of artificial intelligence.

I enjoy exploring how technology works, identifying everyday problems, and turning ideas into useful solutions. For me, programming is more than writing code—it is a way to think, experiment, create, and make things better.

I am still learning, and I believe that staying curious is one of the most valuable skills a developer can have.

## What I am interested in

- **Software Development** — understanding systems and building practical software.
- **Artificial Intelligence** — exploring AI, its capabilities, limitations, and real-world applications.
- **Engineering & Problem-Solving** — breaking complex problems into manageable parts.
- **UI/UX & Design** — making technology clear, accessible, and enjoyable to use.
- **Technology & Innovation** — learning how things work and imagining how they could work better.

## My approach

I believe good ideas should be tested, not just discussed. I learn by researching, building, experimenting, making mistakes, and improving the result.

A few principles guide the way I work:

- Start with the problem, not the technology.
- Prefer useful solutions over unnecessary complexity.
- Learn the fundamentals instead of blindly following trends.
- Treat failure as feedback and keep improving.
- Build responsibly, with people and real needs in mind.

## A belief I follow

> **“Necessity is the mother of invention.”**

And when an attempt does not work, it is not necessarily the end—it can be the beginning of a better approach.

## Beyond the screen

Technology is a field I am passionate about, but I also value education, thoughtful learning, creativity, and personal growth. My long-term goal is to become an engineer and innovator who uses knowledge to create meaningful, practical impact.
